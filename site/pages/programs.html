FAQ

Questions plant leaders ask first.

Where does our data live?

Wherever you decide. Data residency is your call: deploy on-premise on edge gateways, line-side compute or private plant servers, or in your own private cloud. Either way it stays within your firewall, with no server-side logging of proprietary parameters, no training on your production data, and no third-party broker in the pipeline.

How long until we see a working pilot?

Typically 4–8 weeks from scoping to a working pilot on one line.

Do we need to replace our existing systems?

No. Programs integrate with your existing PLCs, SCADA, MES/ERP and CMMS/EAM systems.

We're in a regulated plant. Can you support validation?

Yes. Qualification protocols, audit trails and traceability are completed before a pilot touches live production.

What if our use case isn't listed?

Use "Scope your use case." We'll map it to an existing program or scope a new one.
