Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to talk to you about your project, and we never sell it or track you across the web.

Effective 1 November 2026

Who we are

This website, incolo.si, is run by Incolo Systems, Faridabad, Haryana, India. For anything about this policy or your data, email business@incolosystems.com.

What we collect

  • When you book a scoping call: your name, work email, company, designation, the challenge you describe, the time you choose and, if you add them, your phone number and program of interest.
  • When you email or call us: whatever you choose to send.
  • When you browse: our web server keeps standard technical logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, time) to keep the site secure and working.

We don't use analytics, advertising or third-party tracking scripts, and the site sets no cookies for visitors. The only cookie is a sign-in cookie for the site owner's admin area.

How we use it

  • To schedule your call, send you the calendar invitation and Google Meet link, and prepare for the conversation.
  • To follow up on your enquiry and, if you go ahead, work with you.
  • To protect the site from abuse.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Google Calendar and Google Meet

Booking uses the Google Calendar API through Incolo Systems' own Google account. When you book, the site checks that account's calendar for busy times and creates the meeting there with a Google Meet link. Your name, email and booking details are stored in that calendar event, and Google processes them under the Google Privacy Policy.

The site never asks you to sign in with Google and never accesses your Google account or calendar. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who else sees it

  • The Incolo Systems team members who attend or prepare for your call.
  • Service providers that run the site for us: our hosting provider, our email provider (which delivers the invitation) and Google (calendar and Meet).
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and enquiry details for as long as we need them to follow up and maintain a business relationship with you, and server logs for a limited time. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to us using it, by emailing business@incolosystems.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, stores booking records on our own server with restricted access, and keeps all access keys out of the website code.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date. Use of the site is also covered by our Terms of Service.
